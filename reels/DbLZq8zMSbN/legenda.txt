10 agentic AI concepts you need before 2027 

People who’ve never written a single line of code have full mouth of agent loops and systems that make them 7 figures while they sleep. But here’s the knowledge that survives contact with production in 2027.

1. Harness engineering — designing the environment your agent acts inside: the sandbox, the file system, the runtime surface.

2. Loop engineering — deciding when the agent runs again, and when it stops.

3. Context engineering — getting the right information into the window and keeping the wrong information out.

4. Tool design — your agent is only as capable as the tools you hand it, and only as safe as their worst edge case.

5. Memory architecture — what the agent remembers, for how long, and what it's allowed to forget.

6. Orchestration patterns — one agent, a planner, or a swarm; each fails differently.

7. Guardrails & permissions — what happens when the agent tries something it shouldn't.

8. Evals for agents — you can't test a trajectory the way you test a function.

9. Human-in-the-loop design — knowing which decisions never get delegated.

10. Observability & tracing — when it breaks at step 14 of 30, you need to see step 14.
